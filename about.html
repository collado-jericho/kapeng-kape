About Us

Jericho Collado

I'm the owner of Kapeng Kape, a small coffee shop serving locally sourced Filipino coffee blends.

I started Kapeng Kape because I wanted to share the coffee I grew up with — the strong, no-nonsense barako blends from Batangas — with people who'd never had them before. Most cafes push imported beans, but I wanted a shop that put local farmers and local flavor first.

What began as brewing for friends and neighbors grew into Kapeng Kape. Every cup is still made the same way: sourced locally, brewed with care, and served with the same pride I had on day one.
