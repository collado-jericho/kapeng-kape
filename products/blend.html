Barako Blend

Barako Blend is our signature Batangas-style brew, made from robusta beans grown by small local farmers in the highlands of Batangas. It's bold, full-bodied, and unmistakably strong — the kind of coffee that wakes you up with the first sip. We roast it dark to bring out its deep, slightly smoky flavor, and serve it black so the bean does all the talking.

If you've grown up with Filipino coffee culture, this is the taste you remember. If you haven't, it's a good place to start.
