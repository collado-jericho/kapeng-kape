Iced Kape Espresso

Iced Kape Espresso is a straight espresso shot poured over ice — no milk, no syrup, just cold and concentrated coffee flavor. It's built for hot afternoons when you still want the full punch of our beans without the heat of a regular cup.

We pull each shot fresh per order and pour it immediately, so the ice doesn't dilute the espresso before it hits your table.
