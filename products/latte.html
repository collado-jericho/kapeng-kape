Kapeng Barako Latte

Kapeng Barako Latte takes our bold Batangas-style espresso and softens it with steamed milk, giving you the strength of barako without losing approachability. It's the drink we recommend to anyone who loves the flavor of local coffee but wants something creamier and easier to sip through the afternoon.

We pull the espresso shot strong on purpose, so even with the milk, the barako character still comes through in every sip.

Spec Detail
Base Barako espresso shot
Milk Steamed fresh milk
Serving size 12 oz
